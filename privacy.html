Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how TimeScape Word Puzzle by Yosh (“TimeScape”) uses information. Developer and contact: Yosh, sora.openai1987@gmail.com.

Offline play and device storage

The game stores puzzle progress, player name, settings, collected time, deposits, claim history, and pending reward or lesson states on the device. Offline play does not require a Google account. Signing out retains local game progress. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove local records that have not been backed up.

Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase process authentication. TimeScape receives a basic account profile, including your Google account identifier, name, email address, and a profile-picture URL when supplied. This identifies your account, displays your signed-in identity, and enables account-based cloud features. TimeScape does not receive your Google password and does not request access to Gmail messages, contacts, Google Drive files, or calendars for game login.

Authentication tokens are kept in encrypted native storage using Android Keystore. Do not share account tokens or passwords with the developer.

Cloud progress and reward verification

For signed-in cloud features, Supabase stores an account-linked progress snapshot and submitted reward events. These can include game level and run identifiers, puzzle answers, bonus words, selected time wallet, reward-event identifiers, and server timestamps. They are used to restore progress, resolve sync conflicts, check puzzle submissions, prevent duplicate ranking rewards, and calculate scores. Offline submissions wait on the phone until they can sync.

Progress backups can include the wallet and claim history. A local wallet record or parent acknowledgement is not a server-confirmed Family Link extension. Server answer checks do not prove that a human personally solved a puzzle.

Rankings and public information

Your chosen ranking nickname and verified score can be visible to other signed-in players in Daily, Weekly, and All-time rankings. Choose a nickname that does not reveal a child’s full name or other personal details. Email addresses are not shown on the ranking board. Daily and weekly ranking boundaries use Philippine time; offline points count in the period when the server receives and verifies them.

Services, hosting, and diagnostics

Google provides account authentication. Supabase provides authentication, cloud storage, and server processing. This information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers may process connection information, such as IP addresses, browser or device information, request times, and technical logs, as part of delivering and securing their services. Their own privacy policies also apply: Google, Supabase, and GitHub.

This static information website has no sign-in form, advertising scripts, or developer-added analytics. The game’s dictionary and bundled translation lessons are looked up locally; text-to-speech uses the phone’s selected speech engine, whose processing and network behavior depend on that engine.

How information is used and shared

Account and game information is used to operate sign-in, backups, rankings, reward checks, support, and service security. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Data is shared with the service providers described above to provide these features, with other players only as described for rankings, or when required by applicable law.

Retention and deletion requests

Local information remains until cleared by the user or app storage is removed. Cloud account data, backups, and verified reward records are retained to provide account continuity and score history until deletion is requested or the service is retired, subject to security, backup, and legal requirements.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your TimeScape cloud data, email sora.openai1987@gmail.com from the account used to sign in. Identity confirmation may be needed. A parent or guardian may contact Yosh about a child’s information. Removing Google access alone does not delete existing TimeScape cloud records. Google access can be reviewed at Google Account connections.

Children and parent supervision

Parents or guardians should supervise children’s use, account setup, ranking nicknames, and time claims. Use online features only when permitted by Google’s account rules and applicable age or consent requirements. If a child’s data was provided without required permission, contact Yosh to request review and removal. TimeScape does not ask for a child’s precise location, camera, microphone recordings, private messages, or accessibility access.

Policy updates

Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. Material app changes may also require a new in-app notice. Contact Yosh using the email above with any privacy questions.
