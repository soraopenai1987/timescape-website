Optional Google sign-in supports cloud progress backup and Daily, Weekly, and All-time rankings. Newly submitted puzzle rewards are checked on the server before they count toward verified ranking points.

Time rewards are records for a parent to honor manually. TimeScape does not unlock a phone, override Google Family Link, or automatically extend another app’s time limit. Rewards have no cash value.
